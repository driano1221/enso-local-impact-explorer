One ocean, one planet

ENSO begins in the Pacific. Its influence can reach further.

El Niño–Southern Oscillation is a natural variation of the ocean–atmosphere system in the tropical Pacific. It changes the distribution of warm water, wind circulation, and rainfall and temperature patterns in different parts of the world.
